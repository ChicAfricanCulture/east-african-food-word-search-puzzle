# East African Cuisine Word Search Puzzle

A word search with 30 East African dishes, from ugali and pilau to injera, matoke and Zanzibar pizza. As you play, a small "Kitchen Notes" bar shows how to say each dish and a line about it. *Before you cook it, learn to say it.*

**▶ Play it here: [https://chicafricanculture.github.io/east-african-food-word-search-puzzle/](https://chicafricanculture.github.io/east-african-food-word-search-puzzle/)**

A free game from [The African Gourmet](https://www.theafricangourmet.com/), made to help people learn about African food through play.

## How to play

- Find each dish from the list in the grid. Words can run forward, backward or diagonally.
- Found dishes light up in the grid and are crossed off the list.
- You get 3 hints. There's also a dark mode.
- Tap the Kitchen Notes bar to pause it.
